E-commerce Transformation for Local Retailer

A complete digital overhaul increasing online sales by 200% within the first quarter.

Project Overview

The client, a prominent local hardware retailer, was struggling with an outdated online presence and sluggish sales. They needed a robust, high-converting e-commerce platform that reflected their brand's reliability and extensive inventory.

Our approach focused on streamlining the user journey, optimizing product discoverability, and implementing a seamless checkout process tailored for both B2B and B2C customers.
