---

## 2. Objektif Website

- **Objektif Utama:** Jana inquiry berkualiti → conversion utama = klik WhatsApp / hantar contact form
- **Secondary Actions:** View portfolio/case study, follow social, subscribe update
- **Success Criteria (cadangan):**
  - 10+ WhatsApp inquiry / bulan dalam 3 bulan pertama
  - Bounce rate < 60%, masa di page > 1 minit
  - 3 case study yang meyakinkan dipaparkan

---

## 3. Audience

**Disahkan:** Agensi — target perlukan contact  
**Andaian (perlu sahkan):**
- **Primary:** SME owners (1-20 staff) di Malaysia, umur 25-45, aktif di WhatsApp/Facebook/LinkedIn
- **Secondary:** Freelancer & solo entrepreneur yang nak scale (audience Solo Empire)
- **Geografi:** Malaysia (fokus Lembah Klang dahulu)
- **Bahasa:** Dwibahasa — Melayu utama, English secondary

**Masalah audience (dari Solo Empire cases):**
- Susah hire / susah close sales / susah faham cukai & operasi
- Nak proof cepat sebelum bayar mahal

---

## 4. Positioning & Differentiator

**Andaian:**
- **Positioning:** Agensi proof-first — tak jual janji kosong, tunjuk bukti & test kecil dulu (RM200-500) sebelum komitmen besar
- **Differentiator:**
  1. Framework Solo Empire (proof-first) — bukan terus bina produk besar
  2. Offer kecil & jelas (1 masalah, 1 harga, 1 hasil)
  3. Faham konteks SME Malaysia

**Tone (andaian):** Direct, friendly, Melayu santai profesional — bukan korporat kaku, bukan hype overclaim
